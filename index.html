About Us

SkillSwap is built for peer-to-peer learning where knowledge flows both ways. We remove money from the equation and make collaboration the core value.

  • Peer-to-Peer Learning: Everyone can be both a teacher and a learner.
  • No Money System: A credit-based exchange keeps access fair and inclusive.
  • Community Driven: Learners shape the ecosystem by sharing, mentoring, and supporting each other.
Built for college communities and ambitious creators.

Whether you want to teach coding, public speaking, editing, or math, SkillSwap helps you grow your network while growing your skills.
